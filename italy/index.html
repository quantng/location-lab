Where Italy's industry clusters, and where incentives could seed new ones

ISTAT industrial districts are drawn as cluster footprints. Each province gets a white-space score: the population living outside any cluster, how little of its land a cluster covers, and how close it is to an existing cluster. Click a province for the suggested type of incentive.

Boundaries: openpolis/ISTAT. Cluster footprints approximate each district's local labour system as municipalities within 15 km of its main comune. Provincial-level ZES and crisis-area flags are indicative.

Method and data sources

Clusters: ISTAT, Distretti industriali 2011 (141 districts on SLL 2011, Censimento industria e servizi 2011, released 24 Feb 2015): https://www.istat.it/it/archivio/150320 - file https://www.istat.it/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Elenco-distretti-industriali-2001-2011.zip (Elenco distretti industriali 2011.xls); province siglas matched from comune names via https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matteocontrini/comuni-json/master/comuni.json, with 2011-vintage overrides (Tempio Pausania=OT; Sassocorvaro=PU, now Sassocorvaro Auditore).. Each district is placed at its main municipality and its footprint is the union of municipalities whose centroid lies within 15 km (an approximation of the ISTAT local labour system). Population: ISTAT POSAS resident population, 1 January 2025 (final), joined by ISTAT municipal code; national total 58,943,464. White-space score (0–100) uses percentile ranks: 40% population outside cluster footprints and 35% share of provincial land not covered by a cluster, rescaled to 100. Tiers: Established cluster means two or more districts, or at least 35% of the province's land covered. White space: spillover-ready means a score of at least 55 and a cluster within 60 km. White space: greenfield means a score of at least 55 with no cluster nearby. Emerging cluster means one district. ZES Unica includes the eight Mezzogiorno regions plus Marche and Umbria (from 1 January 2026, under the 2026 budget law). Crisis-area flags (Aree di crisi industriale complessa, MIMIT) were compiled at province level and should be checked against the current MIMIT list before use. A manufacturing employment share was not included in this version.

Download layers: industrial_clusters.geojson, whitespace_zones.geojson, provinces_whitespace_scored.geojson and italy_cluster_whitespace.gpkg (all three layers) are supplied with this map.
