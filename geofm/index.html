What this is

A practitioner's yardstick for geospatial foundation-model (GeoFM) embedding products: does a product cover my area and years, does near mean near, can it predict what the ground looks like now, does the vector's movement mean real change, does a probe trained in one city hold in another, and what does it cost to use. The evaluator shows what was measured for nine areas (Gurgaon, Jaipur, Gurugram, Mumbai, Songadh; London, Dubai, Zürich, a cocoa producer region) across 2017–2025; the live tab reads AlphaEarth tiles from the open bucket for any box you draw; the experiment tab lets you fit and audit your own probe.

Products

Google AlphaEarth Foundations V1 (10 m, 64-d, annual 2017–2025, CC-BY 4.0, open on Google Cloud Storage and the Source Cooperative mirror), TESSERA v1.1 (10 m, 128-d, CC-BY-SA, AWS Open Data), Major TOM embeddings (10 km cells, Hugging Face), Clay v1.5 / LGND, Earth Index v7 and Copernicus-Embed (inventoried). Labels: ESA WorldCover 2020/2021, JRC GHSL built-up surface. Imagery: Sentinel-2 L2A and Sentinel-1.

Method

Embeddings are pooled to H3 resolution 8 (resolution 9 for the two SSDMI areas). Regressions are ridge probes on standardised dimensions with five spatially blocked folds and block-bootstrap intervals; random splits appear only in the leakage audit. Explainability is visual (principal components as colour on fixed axes, displacement maps, UMAP drift, dimension-versus-imagery heat maps) and non-visual (probe coefficients, permutation importance, concept alignment, prototypes and counterfactuals).
