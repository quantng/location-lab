What file do I need?

One row per place. Places can be polygons (tracts, wards, districts), points (stores, branches, sites) or H3 hexagons (a column named h3).

Numbers for the statistics. One column to explain (sales, price, footfall, default rate) and a few to explain it with (income, distance, density…). At least 30 places; 200+ is better.

Optional: satellite embeddings. Columns A00–A63 (AlphaEarth, exported from Google Earth Engine) switch on look-alikes, teach-the-map and the embedding step of the random forest. The deck has a ready prompt to export them.

Optional: a name column lets the Place names tab find your places in text.

Download a sample to see the format: LA tracts (GeoJSON) · Gurugram hexagons (CSV) · Gurugram places (CSV)
